# Competitive Battle Card — Testflow vs. Cybersecurity Market

Quick-reference for live discovery calls and demos. Full strategic context is
in `competitive-risk-mitigation.md`.

---

## One-line positioning

> "Testflow gets you from 'we have nothing' to a shareable remediation plan in
> one 15-minute call — no contract, no implementation project, no security
> hire required."

Use this verbatim when a prospect asks "what makes you different?"

---

## Segment-by-segment responses

### vs. Vanta / Drata / Secureframe / Sprinto / Thoropass

**Their strength:** Continuous compliance monitoring, audit-ready evidence
packs, broad integration catalogs, established auditor relationships.

**Their weakness for your ICP:** Months of onboarding, annual contracts,
require someone internal to own the tool. A 30-person company with no
security hire gets overwhelmed before they get value.

**What to say:**
> "Vanta is the right answer if you're committing to SOC 2 and have someone
> who'll run it. Right now, you need an answer to *this* questionnaire by
> *this date* — that's the problem we solve. If you outgrow us, we'll tell you
> that honestly."

**Never:** Claim you do everything Vanta does. That's a losing argument and a
trust-breaker.

---

### vs. Spreadsheets / "we'll figure it out"

This is the most common real competitor — not software.

**Their 'strength':** Free, already started, no new vendor to approve.

**Their weakness:** Doesn't survive the second questionnaire or the first
audit. The founder answering from memory on a call is a liability, not a plan.

**What to say:**
> "What you have now works until someone asks a follow-up question or wants
> to audit your answers. This gives you a system — the same answers, every
> time, with a remediation list so you can actually fix the gaps."

---

### vs. One-time security consultant

**Their strength:** Personal relationship, custom advice, credentialed.

**Their weakness:** Expensive per hour, slow to schedule, nothing the team
can re-run between engagements.

**What to say:**
> "A consultant is great for complex compliance programs. What you need today
> is a starting point your team can use tomorrow — before you need a
> consultant."

---

### vs. Point-tool questionnaire automation (response libraries)

**Their strength:** Speeds up answering known questionnaires with a
pre-populated library.

**Their weakness:** Assumes you already know your own security posture. If
you can't answer the assessment honestly, a response library surfaces that
gap, it doesn't close it.

**What to say:**
> "Those tools help you answer faster once you know where you stand. This
> helps you figure out where you stand — which usually has to come first."

## Objection quick-reference

| Objection | One-line response |
|---|---|
| "We're looking at Vanta/Drata." | "They're great for teams running a formal audit. If that's you, I'll say so and we can stop here. If you need to answer *this* questionnaire by *this* date, let me show you how fast this takes." |
| "No budget right now." | "The cost here is the deal or renewal you can't close without a security answer — what's that worth?" |
| "We want to think about it." | "Totally fair. What question would an answer to unblock the decision?" |
| "Can this do SOC 2?" | "It gets you the remediation roadmap that a SOC 2 audit will ask about — it's not audit-evidence automation. If you need a badge for a compliance page, Vanta is the right tool and I'll tell you that." |
| "You're too small / new." | "The assessment output is the same whether we've been running five months or five years. What specifically would you need to see to trust it?" |

---

## Disqualify cleanly — don't chase these

Per `gtm/icp.md`, walk away (or redirect to Vanta/Drata) when:

- They already have a GRC platform running.
- They need continuous audit-evidence packs and have a compliance team to
  run it.
- They're a regulated enterprise (bank, hospital) with formal compliance
  mandates.

Saying "you need a different tool" builds trust and generates referrals.
Chasing it burns time and delivers a bad customer experience.
