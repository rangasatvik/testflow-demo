# Ideal customer profile

## Primary target

- **Company size**: 15–150 employees. Small enough that there's no dedicated
  security/compliance hire; large enough that "we'll get to it" has started
  costing them deals.
- **Buying trigger**: a customer, investor, or insurer has just asked for
  proof of security practices (a security questionnaire, SOC 2 request, or
  cyber-insurance renewal) and they have nothing to send back.
- **Titles to reach**: founder/CEO, COO, or Head of Ops at companies without a
  security lead. At slightly larger companies: VP Eng or Head of IT.
- **Verticals**: B2B SaaS, fintech-adjacent, healthtech-adjacent, and
  agencies/consultancies handling client data — anyone who sells into
  enterprises that run vendor security reviews.

## Disqualifiers (don't send to these)

- Already has a dedicated security/compliance hire or an existing GRC vendor
  (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, etc.) — different pitch, different sequence.
- Pre-revenue / pre-customer companies with no vendor-review pressure yet.
- Regulated enterprises (banks, hospitals) needing a full compliance
  platform, not a lightweight assessment tool.

## Sourcing

- LinkedIn Sales Navigator filtered by headcount + title, cross-referenced
  against companies that publicly mention "SOC 2," "security questionnaire,"
  or "vendor review" pain (job posts for a first compliance hire are a strong
  signal).
- Warm intros from the founder's existing network first — founder-led sales
  should start with people who'll take the call regardless of the pitch.
