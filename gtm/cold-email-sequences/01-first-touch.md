## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** keep the personalisation line short and factual — no flattery.
- **Do** ask a single yes/no question in the first paragraph — it lowers the friction to reply.
- **Don't** attach a pitch deck or PDF — attachments kill deliverability.
- **Don't** use "I hope this email finds you well" or any opener that signals mass email.
- **Don't** CC anyone else on the first touch.
