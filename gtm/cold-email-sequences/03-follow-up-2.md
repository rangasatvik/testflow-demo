## Notes

- "Break-up" emails consistently have the highest reply rate in the sequence — keep the tone light, not desperate.
- The two concrete options (self-serve or book later) give the prospect a low-friction way to say yes without committing to a live call.
- After this email, move the prospect to a "nurture" list. Re-engage in 60 days if they hit a relevant trigger (job post, funding round, LinkedIn post about compliance).
